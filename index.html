Frequently asked questions

What is HaishinKit?

HaishinKit is a family of open-source libraries for adding live audio and video streaming to your app. Use HaishinKit.swift for Apple platforms, HaishinKit.kt for Android, or HaishinKit.dart for Flutter.

Which streaming protocols are supported?

All three SDKs support RTMP(S), SRT, WHIP, and WHEP. RTMP(S) and SRT support publishing and playback. WHIP is for publishing, and WHEP is for playback.

Can I use HaishinKit in a commercial app?

The BSD 3-Clause license permits commercial use under its terms. Check your SDK’s repository for its license and the licenses of its dependencies.

Where can I find documentation for older versions?

The documentation version list has links to older releases. You can also replace latest in the URL with a published version number.

About HaishinKit

The name combines Haishin, the Japanese word for streaming in this context, with Kit, short for SDK. HaishinKit began as an iPhone project in 2015 and now offers libraries for Apple platforms, Android, and Flutter. It supports both live publishing and playback.
